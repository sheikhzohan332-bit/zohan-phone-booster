Privacy Policy – ZOHAN Security

Developer: Talha Computer · Last updated: 23 November 2026

ZOHAN Security ("the app") is a phone security and maintenance tool. This policy explains what the app accesses on your phone and what it does with it. In short: the app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. Everything is processed on your phone. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Network use

The app uses the internet only for features you start yourself:

  • Speed Test – downloads and uploads test data with Cloudflare's speed test service (speed.cloudflare.com).
  • Network Diagnostics – contacts connectivitycheck.gstatic.com (Google) and 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare) to measure latency and show your public IP address. The IP address is shown to you only.
  • Link Checker "Expand" – only when you tap it, the app asks the website in the pasted link where it redirects to (no page content is loaded). That website will see a normal request from your phone's IP address.

These services receive the technical data any internet request includes (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies. The app sends no personal data, identifiers or app lists to them or to the developer.

3. Data sharing and selling

The app does not share data with third parties and does not sell data. There are no advertising or analytics libraries in the app.

4. Data retention and deletion

All data stays in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete scan history in Settings › Clear scan history, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. The developer has no copy to delete.

5. Permissions you control

Usage access, notification access and location are optional. The app works without them; only the related feature is limited. You can turn them off at any time in Android Settings.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
